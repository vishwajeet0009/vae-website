VAE — Vishwajeet Automotive Explorer
======================================
This is a static, multi-page VAE website starter.

Files:
- index.html — homepage
- bikes.html — bikes
- cars.html — cars
- comparisons.html — comparisons
- technology.html — technology
- news.html — news
- mods.html — mods & gear
- about.html — about
- contact.html — contact
- style.css — shared design

How to preview:
Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.

Before publishing:
1. Replace placeholder contact email.
2. Replace generic demo articles with verified VAE content.
3. Add your final logo and licensed/original images.
4. Connect the contact/newsletter forms to a real service.
5. Add privacy policy, terms and cookie/analytics choices as appropriate.
6. Connect your real Instagram/YouTube/Facebook URLs.
7. Buy/connect your preferred domain and hosting.

The design uses the VAE Black + Red + White brand direction and generic/original vehicle illustrations rather than manufacturer imagery.
