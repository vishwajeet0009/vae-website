Pulsar N160 vs Apache RTR 160 4V

Two popular 160cc street bikes compared on performance, technology, braking, suspension and everyday usability.

VAE Comparison · Updated August 2026

The Bajaj Pulsar N160 and TVS Apache RTR 160 4V target riders who want a sporty 160cc motorcycle without moving into a larger-engine class. The numbers below use current manufacturer information available for the 2026 models.

Quick specifications

Performance

On headline output, the Apache has the advantage: TVS lists 17.55 PS in Sport mode versus 16 PS for the N160, while torque is also slightly higher at 14.73 Nm versus 14.65 Nm. That gives the Apache a stronger performance-oriented specification sheet, particularly for riders who enjoy higher-rpm riding.

Engine character

The N160 uses a 164.82 cc oil-cooled single-cylinder engine, while the Apache uses a 159.7 cc oil-cooled four-valve engine. In simple terms, the Apache's four-valve layout and higher peak-rpm output support its sportier character, while the N160 keeps its focus on a straightforward street-bike package.

Technology and features

The 2026 Apache RTR 160 4V adds features including a projector headlamp, Assist & Slipper Clutch and, on the higher-end TFT variant, traction control and a 5-inch connected TFT display. TVS lists three main 2026 variants with different equipment levels.

Bajaj's current N160 page highlights features such as dual-channel ABS, turn-by-turn navigation, call controls, a USB charging port and, on select variants, USD forks.

Braking and suspension

TVS lists a 270 mm front petal disc and different rear-brake configurations by variant, with USD suspension on the dual-channel ABS with USD and TFT versions. Bajaj lists disc brakes and offers USD forks on select N160 variants. The exact equipment therefore depends on the variant you compare.

Which one should you choose?

Choose the Apache RTR 160 4V if your priority is outright performance, sportier technology and a more performance-focused feature set.

Choose the Pulsar N160 if you prefer its 164.82 cc street-bike package, 14-litre tank and the ergonomics/features offered by the variant you select.

VAE verdict: On current published specifications, the Apache wins the performance and technology battle; the N160 remains a strong choice for riders looking for a balanced everyday street motorcycle.
